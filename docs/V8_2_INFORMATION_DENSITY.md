# V8.2 information-density notes

## Compact hierarchy

- Home: one prominent continue action; word-of-day is surfaced earlier.
- Vocabulary: list/filter/search stays in the main scan path; learning chart is secondary/collapsible.
- Decks: creation no longer dominates the list; secondary actions are grouped; delete/archive controls are less visually prominent and archive remains reversible.
- Review: compact due-count summary; 5/10/15/20 selector and exercise type remain available when the due queue exists.
- HSK: level selector and three learning destinations remain clear without stacking full-height hero actions.
- Exam: supported modes and recent attempts are grouped; setup has a single clear start action; explanations are secondary until review.
- Grammar/reader: content cards lead; extra definitions/context are disclosed or below the main content.
- Shared Chinese route wrapper reduces duplicated headers/vertical whitespace and includes the bottom safe edge. English keeps the original shared `Screen` defaults.

Reference captures are in `artifacts/v8-2-chinese-redesign-2026-10-14/gallery-10-features/`. Screens were reviewed at emulator font scale 1.0 only. Long text and bottom clipping still require large-font and narrow-device checks. Large button targets are intentionally retained for touch accessibility; the refinement reduces redundant full-width actions, not touch target size.
