# V8.2 Exam Center

The HSK tab's Ôn thi segment opens a distinct exam center. It exposes real supported activities and local history without presenting unsupported or draft material as an official mock exam. Setup reuses the existing exam repository and session engine; choosing a level/duration creates an actual local attempt.

Sources: `src/components/exam/ExamCenterContent.tsx`, `src/app/exam/index.tsx`, `src/app/exam/setup.tsx`, `src/app/exam/history.tsx`.

## Supported and labeled

- Real question-bank practice for the available reading/vocabulary question families.
- 5/10/15-minute timed practice options; these are practice durations, not official HSK exam durations.
- In-progress attempt resume, local history, explicit submission, result review and explanation.
- Reading/vocabulary skill practice is labeled by what the current bank actually supports.
- Writing/speaking screens are self-practice and are not official HSK/HSKK scoring. No speech grade or certificate is invented.
- Unverified mock coverage is labeled as insufficient data; the bundled content validator currently finds zero verified full mock exams.

Native evidence: QA screenshots 09–30, plus `exam-readiness-confirmed.png`, `50-exam-skills.png`, and `52-exam-speaking.png`. The QA attempt used four real questions. One question had three choices because that is what its authored item contains; distractors were not fabricated to force a fixed number. A/B/C/D presentation is only appropriate where the source question supplies four options.

## Result of the exercised attempt

On the private emulator, the learner answered three of four, with two correct, then explicitly submitted. The result displayed 2/4 (50%), one unanswered question, and the wrong answer's correct choice/rationale. History retained the submitted result. The score was generated by the existing exam engine, not a mock value.
