# V5 observed error intelligence

Sổ lỗi now groups up to 200 unresolved records by persisted skill and explicit confusable exercise type. It shows open items, cumulative wrong attempts, and concrete offline practice guidance. A recurring flag requires an individual item with at least three mistakes; three unrelated one-off mistakes are not a diagnosis. Existing drill links and three unassisted-success resolution behavior remain unchanged. No SRS date or review history is rewritten.

Loading, error/retry and empty states are distinct. Pure tests cover duplicate IDs, resolved/invalid rows, bounded previews and non-inferred categories.

Not implemented: verified tone-pair/semantic/syntax diagnoses, personalized micro-lessons generated from answer-level evidence, cross-session error event history or Gemini evaluation. Current records lack enough evidence for those claims. Skill groups are not official exam-readiness predictions. Device QA remains pending.
