# Data licenses and attribution

The generated offline vocabulary file combines these open datasets:

- **complete-hsk-vocabulary**, copyright Yanis Zafirópulos, MIT License: https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary
- **CVDICT**, Phong Phan, Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0: https://github.com/ph0ngp/CVDICT

Vietnamese definitions derived from CVDICT remain under CC BY-SA 4.0. The app marks these translations as `machine` because CVDICT documents that most definitions were machine-translated and later partially reviewed.

The hand-curated starter examples and Vietnamese gloss corrections in `src/data/starter-vocabulary.ts` are project-authored content.
